## 2. Safeguards Verified

- **P0 Privacy:** `appendStructuredVisualEvent` strips `publicRoleId`, `publicFaction`, and `publicCopiedRoleId` whenever `!actor.revealed && !state.finished`. Antimony / Vengeful Spirit copy cut-ins only trigger for publicly revealed skills; Voodoo puppet pause indicator (`🔒`) reveals zero private card metadata.
- **Reduced Motion & Graphics Quality Tiers:** All 9 Tier-4 Canvas 2D renderers scale particle counts via `particleScale` (`0` on Low tier) and disable sway/shatter particles when `reducedMotion === true`; WAAPI pawn choreography is skipped cleanly under `reducedMotion`.
- **Audio Hygiene:** Every cue runs through `playSoundForVisualEvent` with `event.id` deduplication, category cooldown coalescing, global mute toggle, `MAX_ACTIVE_VOICES` capping, and `stopAllBoardGameSounds()` on skip/unmount.
